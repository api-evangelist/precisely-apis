Key Takeaways The context layer for AI isn’t one product, but a combination of metadata, semantics, and data quality that helps AI understand business-specific data. Metadata tells AI what data exists and where; a semantic layer tells AI what that data means. Both are necessary, and neither is sufficient alone.
